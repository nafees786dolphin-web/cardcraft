# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-27)

First release (the MVP from the PRD).

- Four-step flow (Details, Style, Personalise, Download) with a live front and back preview
- Six templates: Classic, Modern Left, Bold Band, Split, Minimal Mono, Corporate
- Logo upload (PNG, JPG, SVG, up to 5 MB) in an optional rounded-rectangle frame, with initials used when there's no logo
- 8 accent colours plus a custom colour; text colours adjust automatically to stay readable
- 3 font styles, 3 corner styles (default: Rounded) and 3 card sizes (default: India 90 × 54 mm)
- Long text shrinks to fit, with a warning if it still doesn't fit
- PDF download (600 DPI) and PNG download (300 DPI), front and back
- Progress saved in the browser, plus a two-tap "Start over"
- Works both inside Claude and as a standalone web page (GitHub Pages or opened as a file)
